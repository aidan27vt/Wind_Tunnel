About

The project

This is a from-scratch build of a small open-circuit suction wind tunnel — a benchtop-scale rig for visualizing airflow over models using discrete smoke lines. The goal is a tunnel with clean enough flow that the smoke stays crisp through the test section, so aerodynamic behavior is actually legible rather than a blur.

Every part is modeled in SOLIDWORKS and fabricated on a 3D printer, section by section. I'm documenting the whole thing as a live build log: what's designed, what's printed, and the reasoning behind the design choices along the way.

I'm a student building this on my own to go deeper on applied aerodynamics and fabrication than coursework alone allows. Replace this line with a bit about yourself.
