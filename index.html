Open-Circuit Suction · Subsonic Speeds · Smoke Visualization

Designing & constructing a subsonic
wind tunnel from scratch

A personal project open-circuit suction wind tunnel with a smoke rake for flow visualization. Each component is designed & modeled in SOLIDWORKS, and is constructed with a 3D printer. This is a live build log that will continue to be updated as the build progresses.

Component notes

Short write-ups on the pieces that are furthest along.

Honeycomb straightener

3D-printed in sections and glued together. Straightens incoming flow and knocks down large-scale swirl before the contraction.

missing imageimages/render-contraction.png

Contraction chamber

The contraction ratio sets how much the flow accelerates and how uniform the test-section velocity profile ends up. Profile chosen to avoid separation along the walls.
